# Pricing cards — 24px gaps, always

> Pricing tiers use **24px gaps** — between stacked tier cards and between each price and its CTA. Depends on: `cards.md`, `spacing.md`.

Per `SKILL.md` and `cards.md`:

1. **Stacked pricing tiers** (any vertical pricing card list) use a **`spacing-6` (24px)** gap between cards — never `spacing-4` (16px) or an ad-hoc value.
2. **Price → CTA inside each card** — **`spacing-6` (24px)** between the price line and the tier's button, so the buttons align row-to-row across tiers.

## Applies to

- The pricing section on the marketing landing page.
- Any future pricing grid that stacks tier cards vertically.

## Before shipping

Confirm the tier-stack gap and each price→CTA spacing measure **24px** in the browser.
